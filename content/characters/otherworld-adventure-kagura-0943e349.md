## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 170% of ATK to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants all Technique-type allies &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Overwhelming Sword Strike
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to inflict Silence and &#91;Top&#93; Critical DEF Decrease on the target for 5 sec, and reduces the currently accumulated Ultimate Skill Gauge by 0.9 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Heavy Swordsmanship: &quot;Whirl&quot; Form
<p>Every 10 sec, deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to enemies within the target area. After the attack hits, has a 40% chance to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Bleed and &#91;Top&#93; Burn on each target for 5 sec.</p>
