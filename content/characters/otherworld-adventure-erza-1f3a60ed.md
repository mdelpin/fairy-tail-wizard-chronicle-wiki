## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 160% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to  inflict &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Decrease on the target for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Astral Break
<p>After a normal skill hits, if an enemy within the target area is afflicted with Paralysis, Burn, or Bleed, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Rate Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease on each affected enemy for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Earth-Splitting Roaring Slash
<p>Every 10 sec, deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to enemies within the target area. After the attack hits, inflicts Heal Block and &#91;Top&#93; Bleed on each target for 7 sec. Extends the duration of the additional effects by 0.5 sec for each Bravery-type ally (max 3 sec).</p>
