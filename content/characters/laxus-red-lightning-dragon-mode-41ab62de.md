## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 160% of ATK as damage to the target. If a critical hit occurs, grants allies of the Skill attribute &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 3 seconds.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Thunder Dragon Fangtian Halberd
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 20% chance to deal 350% damage to the target. If the attack hits a target afflicted with &quot;Bleed&quot;, deals an additional 350% of ATK as damage.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Megaton: Red Lightning
<p>Deals 900% of ATK as damage to enemies within range every 10 seconds. After a successful hit, has a 50% chance to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Bleed, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease on each target for 5 seconds.</p>
