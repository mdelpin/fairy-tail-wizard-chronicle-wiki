## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 170% of ATK to the target. When a Critical Hit occurs, applies the following to Heart-type allies for 3 seconds: &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Lightning Fire Dragon&#x27;s Firing Hammer
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to deal 400% damage to the target. After the attack hits, applies the following to the target for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Burn, &#91;Top&#93; Paralysis.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Crimson Lotus: Exploding Lightning Blade
<p>Deals damage equal to 850% of ATK to all enemies every 10 seconds. After the attack hits, if the target is affected by Burn or Paralysis, applies the following to the target for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, Heal Block, Silence.</p>
