## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)

<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to inflict Heal Block and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease on the target for 5 sec.</p>

<p>Every 10 sec, deals 1000% ATK damage to targets within range. Increases damage by 50% for each Technique-type ally (up to 350%). After the attack hits, has a 40% chance for each target to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Bleed and &#91;Top&#93; Burn, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease for 5 sec.</p>
