## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 190% of ATK to the target. When a Critical Hit occurs, grants self &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Sunlight Blessing
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to grant all allies &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase, and &#91;Mid&#93; Shield for 5 sec. Extends the duration by 0.5 sec for each Skill-element ally (up to 3.5 sec).</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Summer Waveblade!
<p>Every 9 sec, deals damage equal to 1000% of ATK to enemies within range. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;High&#93; Burn and &#91;High&#93; Bleed on the target for 5 sec.</p>
