## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 120% ATK damage to the target. After hitting, heals the ally with the lowest HP for 140% of your ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Top Model of Weekly Sorcerer
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to inflict on the target for 4 sec, Silence.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Fairy Tail&#x27;s Poster Girl
<p>Every 10 sec, restore HP to the ally with the lowest HP equal to 850% of your ATK, and grant that ally for 5 sec &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Heal. Then remove all debuffs applied to all allies.</p>
