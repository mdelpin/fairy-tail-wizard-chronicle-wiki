## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 190% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict the target with Silence, &#91;High&#93; ATK Decrease, and &#91;Low&#93; Accuracy Decrease for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Let the Brawl Begin
<p>After a normal skill hits, if an enemy within the target&#x27;s area is affected by Bleed or Silence, inflicts each enemy with &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical DEF Decrease, and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Summer Drunken Brawl
<p>Every 10 sec, deals damage equal to 1000% of ATK to enemies within range. After the attack hits, deals additional damage equal to 50% of the user&#x27;s lost HP to the target, and inflicts &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease for 5 sec.</p>
