## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 190% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to restore own HP by 600% of ATK and grants &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Death Wave
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to deal 400% damage to targets within range. If the target is affected by Burn, Paralysis, Silence, or Blind when the attack hits, deals and additional 400% ATK damage and inflicts &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Decrease for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Ankhseram Black Palm
<p>Deals damage equal to 1050% of ATK to all enemies every 8 sec. After the attack hits, inflicts Blind and Heal Block on the target for 5 sec., and removes all buffs from the target.</p>
