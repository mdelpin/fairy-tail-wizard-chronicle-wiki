## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 120% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores HP of the ally with the lowest HP by 160% of the caster&#x27;s ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Protective Charm
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to apply the following to all allies for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Heal, Action Block Barrier.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Healing Talisman
<p>Every 11 seconds, restores all allies HP by 950% of own ATK. Additionally, grants the target for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Shield and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase, and removes all debuffs from all allies. Furthermore, grants all Brave-attribute allies for 5 seconds: &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Accuracy Increase.</p>
