## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 160% 0f ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Decrease on the target for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Juvia is a Rain Woman
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to deal damage equal to 450% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Burn and Silence on the target for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Water Lock
<p>Deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to enemies within the target area every 10 sec. Damage increases by 50% for each Brave ally (up to 350%). If the target is afflicted with Silence or Burn after the attack hits, inflicts the following effects on each target for 7 sec: &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease,  &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, and Heal Block.</p>
