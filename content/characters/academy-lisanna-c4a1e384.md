## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deal 150% ATK damage to the target. On hit, there is a 20% chance to inflict Bind on the target for 2s.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – After-School Review Meeting
<p>After a normal skill hits, if enemies within the target area are Bound, inflict each enemy for 5s with &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease, and Heal Block.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Blackboard Master
<p>Every 10s, deal 850% ATK damage to targets within range. On hit, inflcit &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease on the target for 5s, and each target has a 40% chance to be inflicted with Bind for 5s.</p>
