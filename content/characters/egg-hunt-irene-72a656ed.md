## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 120% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores HP of the ally with the lowest HP by 160% of the caster&#x27;s ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Spring Enchant
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 30% chance to grant all allies &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase and Action Block Barrier for 5 sec, and advance the current Special Skill Gauge by 0.7 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Resurrection Egg
<p>Every 11 sec, restores HP of all allies by 950% of the caster&#x27;s ATK. Additionally grants &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Heal and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Increase to targets for 5 sec and removes all debuffs from all allies.</p>
