## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants Wisdom allies for 3 sec, &#91;High&#93; DEF Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Resistance Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Torn Fate
<p>After a normal skill hits, if enemies within the target area are affected by Paralysis or Blind, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Decrease on each enemy for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Celestial Magic: Amanogawa
<p>Deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to all enemies every 10 seconds. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict Blind and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease on each target for 5 sec. Increases the duration of the additional effects by 0.5 sec for each Intelligence ally (up to 3 sec).</p>
