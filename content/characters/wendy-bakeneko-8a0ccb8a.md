## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 160% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict &#91;High&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Decrease on the target for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Don&#x27;t Let Your Guard Down, Nya!
<p>If enemies within the target area are afflicted with Paralysis after a normal skill hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease on each target for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Happy! Carla! You&#x27;re Up, Nya!
<p>Every 9 sec, deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to enemies within range. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Paralysis on each target for 5 sec, with a 30% chance to inflict Blind for 5 sec.</p>
