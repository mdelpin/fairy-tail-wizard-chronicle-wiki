## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 180% of ATK to the target. When a Critical Hit occurs, grants all Technique allies &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - White Dragon&#x27;s Holy Breath
<p>After a Normal S kill hits has a 30% chance to deal damage equal 400% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Bleed and Silence on the target for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Holy Ray
<p>Deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to enemies within the target area every 10 sec. Increases damage by 50% for each Technique ally (up to 350%). After the attack hits, if the target is affected by Bleed, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, and Heal Block on the target for 5 sec.</p>
