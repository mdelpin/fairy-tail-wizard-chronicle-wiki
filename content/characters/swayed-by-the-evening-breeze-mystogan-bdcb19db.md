### 🗡️ Special Skill – Moonlit Radiance

### 💥 Ultimate – Moonlit Guidance
<p>Every 10 sec, deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to enemies within the target area. After the attack hits, grants all allies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Shield for 5 sec.
