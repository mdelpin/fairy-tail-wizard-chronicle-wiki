## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 170% of ATK to the target. On critical hit, grants allies with INT attributes for 3s: &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase and &#91;High&#93; Accuracy Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Guide of the Star Spirits
<p>Every 5s, grants allies with INT attribute for 5s: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Star Whip
<p>Deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to targets within range every 9s. After the attack hits, has a 50% chance to apply to each target for 5s: &#91;Top&#93; Paralysis and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease.</p>
