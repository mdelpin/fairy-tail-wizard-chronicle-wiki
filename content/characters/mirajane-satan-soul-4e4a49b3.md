## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. When a critical hit occurs, for 3 seconds, grants allies of the Heart attribute &#91;High&#93; Critical Damage Increase and &#91;High&#93; Evasion Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Demon Blast
<p>Ater a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to deal damage equal to 250% to enemies within the target area. After an attack hits, there is a 50% chance to apply Silence to each target for 5 seconds.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Darkness Stream
<p>Every 10 seconds, deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to enemies within range. If the target is in the Silence state after the attack hits, for each target, for 5 seconds, applies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Decrease, and &#91;Top&#93; Bleed.</p>
