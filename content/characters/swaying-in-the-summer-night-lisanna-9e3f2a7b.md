## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores the HP of the ally with the lowest HP by an amount equal to 130% of the user&#x27;s ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Giant Blossoms Blooming in the Night Sky
<p>Every 5 sec, grants all allies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Firework Blessing
<p>Every 11 sec, restores HP to all allies equal to 950% of the user&#x27;s ATK. Increases healing by 50% for each Technique ally (up to 350%). Also grants the target &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Heal, Action Block Barrier, and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 5 sec, and removes all debuffs from all allies.</p>
