## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 150% ATK damage to the target. After hitting, 20% chance to inflict 1s of Bind.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Deadly Predation
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to deal damage equal to 200% of ATK to all enemies. After the attack hits, there is a 50% chance to inflict for 5 sec &#91;Top&#93; Bleed on each target.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Repent
<p>Every 11 sec, deal damage equal to 850% of ATK to enemies within the target area. After the attack hits, if the target is in Bleed status, inflict on the target for 5 sec &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease and Bind.</p>
