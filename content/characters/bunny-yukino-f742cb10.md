## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deal damage equal to 160% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, there is a 20% chance to grant yourself, for 3 seconds &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Bunny Enchant
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to grant all Heart-element allies, for 5 seconds, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase, and advance the current Ultimate Skill Gauge by 0.7 seconds.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Lucky Rapid Rain
<p>Every 10 seconds, deal damage equal to 850% of ATK to enemies within the target area. After the attack hits, there is a 50% chance per target to apply, for 5 seconds, Silence.</p>
