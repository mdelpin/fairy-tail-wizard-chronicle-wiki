## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 150% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to grant self for 3 seconds: &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Mana Load
<p>Each time you defeat an enemy, grants self for 7 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Natsu the Fireball
<p>Deals 600% damage to all enemies every 10 seconds. After the attack hits, applies to all enemies afflicted with Burn for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease.<br>​</p>
