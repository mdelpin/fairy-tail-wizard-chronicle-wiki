## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 170% ATK damage to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants all Courage-type allies &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Acid Music
<p>After a Normal Skill hits, if enemies within the target&#x27;s range are affected by Paralysis or Burn, inflicts &#91;High&#93; Bleed and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, Heal Block on each enemy for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Star the Rock
<p>Every 10 sec, deals 950% ATK damage to targets within range. After the attack hits,  has a 40% chance for each target to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Critical DEF Decrease and &#91;High&#93; Burn, &#91;High&#93; Freeze for 5 sec. Extends the duration of these additional effects by 0.5 sec for each Courage-type ally (up to 3.5 sec).</p>
