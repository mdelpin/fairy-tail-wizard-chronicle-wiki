## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 150% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, restores the HP of the ally with the lowest HP by 150% of own ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Grand Harvest Feast
<p>Every 7 sec, grants all allies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 5 sec, and advances the current Ultimate Skill Gauge by 0.9 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Fairy Fantasia
<p>Every 11 sec, restores all allies HP by 1000% of Own ATK. increases the healing amount by 50% for each Heart-type ally (up to 350%). Also grants targets &#91;Top&#93; Shield and &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Healing, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase for 5 sec, and removes all debuffs from all allies.</p>
