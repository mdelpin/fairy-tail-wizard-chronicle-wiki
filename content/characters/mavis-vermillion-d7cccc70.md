## Game text

### Normal Attack (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 120% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores HP to the ally with the lowest HP equal to 150% of your ATK.</p>

### Special Skill – A Fairy&#x27;s Wish
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to grant all allies for 5 seconds Action Block Barrier, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase.</p>

### Ultimate – Fairy Sphere
<p>Every 10 seconds, restores HP to all allies equal to 900% of your ATK. Also grants the target for 7 seconds &#91;Top&#93; Continuous Heal and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase. Additionally, removes all debuffs from all allies.</p>
