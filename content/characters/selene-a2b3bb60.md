## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 180% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict &#91;High&#93; Critical Defense Decrease and Silence on the target for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Moonlit Conflagration
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 20% chance to deal 350% damage to enemies within the target area. If the target is afflicted with Burn, Paralysis, Silence, or Bleed after the attack hits, deals additional damage equal to 350% of ATK.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Moonlight Butterflies
<p>Every 11 sec, deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to all enemies. After the attack hits, grants self Invincible, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 5 sec.</p>
