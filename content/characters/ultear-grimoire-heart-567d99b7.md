## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 180% of ATK to the target. When a critical Hit occurs, grants all Brave allies &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Chain of Time
<p>If enemies within the target area are affected by Freeze after a normal skill hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, and Silence on each enemy for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Ice-Make: Rosen Krone
<p>Deals damage equal to 950% of ATK to all enemies every 10 sec. After a successful hit, has a 30% chance to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Freeze on each target for 5 sec. Extends the duration of this additional effect by 0.5 sec for each Brave ally (up to 3 sec).</p>
