## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 180% ATK damage to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants all Intelligence-type allies &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Illusion Magic: Truth-Falsehood Reversal
<p>After a Normal Skill hits, has a 20% chance to deal 450% damage to targets within range. If the target is affected by Burn, Paralysis or Bleed when the attack hits, deals an additional 450% ATK damage and removes all buffs from the target.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Guidance to Victory
<p>Every 10 sec, deals 1000% ATK damage to all enemies. Increases damage by 50% for each Intelligence-type ally (up to 350%). After the attack hits, inflicts Silence and &#91;Top&#93; Bleed, &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease on the target for 5 sec, and sets back the current Ultimate Skill Gauge by 1.2 sec.</p>
