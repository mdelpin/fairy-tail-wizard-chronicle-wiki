## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants INT-type allies for 3 seconds &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Nine Thunder Stars
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 20% chance to deal 400% damage to the target. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Paralysis on the target for 5 seconds.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Seven-Star Sword
<p>Deals damage equal to 750% of ATK to all enemies every 10 seconds. After an attack hits, if the target is afflicted with Paralysis, for 5 seconds, grants the target &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Crititcal Defense Increase, Heal Block.</p>
