## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill
<p>Deals 190% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to restore own HP by 600% of ATK and grants Critical Shield for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Treat For You!
<p>Every 5 sec, grants all Intelligence-type allies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase, &#91;High&#93; Continuous Healing for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Divine Feast of Sweets
<p>Every 8 sec, deals 1050% ATK damage to all enemies. After the attack hits, has a 50% chance per target to inflict Bind and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease for 5 sec, and removes all buffs from the target.</p>
