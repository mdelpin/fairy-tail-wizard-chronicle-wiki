## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 120% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores HP to the ally with the lowest HP equal to 150% of your ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Heartful Gift
<p>Every 7 seconds, grants Technique-type allies for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase and &#91;High&#93; Shield.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Sweet Water Gift
<p>Every 11 seconds, restores HP of all allies equal to 900% of your ATK. Additionally, grants the target for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Increase.</p>
