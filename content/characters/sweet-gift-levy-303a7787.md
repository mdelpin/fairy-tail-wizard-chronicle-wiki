## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals Damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 10% chance to inflict Silence on the target for 3 seconds.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Sweet and Dangerous Love Letter
<p>After a normal skill hits, if the target is affected by Silence, grants for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – 3D Text -Sweet-
<p>Every 9 sec, deal damage equal to 650% of ATK within the target area. After the attack hits,  there is a 50% chance for 3 sec to inflict Silence and rewind the targets accumulated Ultimate Skill gauge by 0.7 seconds.</p>
