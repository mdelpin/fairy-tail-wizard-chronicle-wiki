## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, restores HP of the ally with the lowest HP by 130% of own ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Peaceful Sunset
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to grant all allies for 5 sec, &#91;Top&#93; Shield, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Accuracy Increase, and advances the Special Skill Gauge by 0.7 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Lightning Dragon&#x27;s Rest
<p>Every 11 sec, restores all allies HP by 750% of own ATK. Increases damage by 50% for each INT ally (up to 350%). Also grants the targets &#91;Top&#93; Continous Heal and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase for 5 sec., and removes all debuffs from all allies. Additionally, grants all INT allies &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase and &#91;High&#93; ATK Increase for 5 sec.</p>
