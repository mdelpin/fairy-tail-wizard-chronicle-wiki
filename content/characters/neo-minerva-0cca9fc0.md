## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 160% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to restore own HP by 700% of ATK and grants Critical Shield for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Merciless Demon
<p>When HP falls 50% or below, once only, grants self 30% Damage Reflection Shield and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase,  &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase for 5 sec, and restores HP by 1500% of ATK.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Territory Nihility
<p>Every 8 sec, deals 850% ATK damage to targets within range. After the attack hits, has a 50% chance for each target to inflict &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;High&#93; Burn, Silence for 5 sec</p>
