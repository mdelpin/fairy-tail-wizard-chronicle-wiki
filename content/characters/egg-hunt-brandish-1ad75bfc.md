## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 150% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, there is a 20% chance to inflict Silence on the target for 2 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Egg Diminish
<p>After a normal skill hits, if enemies within the target area are affected by Silence, there is a 5 sec effect applied to each enemy: &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease, and Heal Block.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Command of T Ultra Expand
<p>Deals damage equal to 800% of ATK to all enemies every 9 sec. After the atttack hits, there is a 40% chance for each target to inflict Silence and &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease for 5 sec.</p>
