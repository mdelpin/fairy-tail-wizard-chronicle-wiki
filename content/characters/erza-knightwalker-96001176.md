## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 130% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to grant self for 3 seconds: &#91;High&#93; DEF Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Defense Increase.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Spear Mage
<p>While on the field, grants all Heart allies: &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Increase.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Saint Spear, Ravelt
<p>Deals 850% ATK damage to enemies within range every 9 seconds. After the attack hits,  grants self for 7 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; Shield, &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; DEF Increase. Additionally, has a 50% chance to grant each ally for 5 seconds: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Shield.</p>
