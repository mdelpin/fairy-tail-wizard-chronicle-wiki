## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals damage equal to 170% of ATK to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict on the target for 3s: &#91;High&#93; Burn.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Fire Dragon Slayer
<p>After a normal skill hits, has a 30% chance to grant self for 5s: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase, &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Increase, and &#91;Top&#93; Shield.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Roar of the Fire Dragon
<p>Deals damage equal to 900% of ATK to targets within range every 10s. If the target is afflicted with Burn after the attack hits, applies to each target for 5s: &#91;Top&#93; ATK Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Damage Decrease.</p>
