## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 160% ATK damage to the target. After the attack hits, has a 30% chance to inflict &#91;Top&#93; Critical DEF Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Rate Decrease on the target for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – A Grown-Up&#x27;s Whim
<p>After a Normal Skill hits, if enemies within the target&#x27;s range are affected by Freeze, Burn, or Bleed, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; DEF Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Resistance Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Decrease, &#91;High&#93; Paralysis on each affected enemy for 7 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Enchanting Dagger Wielder
<p>Every 10 sec, deals 900% ATK damage to all enemies. After the attack hits, inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Bleed and Heal Block on the target for 7 sec, and removes all buffs from the target. Extends the duration of these additional effects by 0.5 sec for each Heart-type ally (up to 3 sec)</p>
