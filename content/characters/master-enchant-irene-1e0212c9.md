## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 180% ATK damage to the target. When a critical hit occurs, grants all Technique-type allies &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase and &#91;High&#93; Critical Damage Increase for 3 sec.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill – Deus Zero
<p>After Normal Skill hits, has a 30% chance to deal 450% damage to targets within range. If the target is affected by Burn, Silence, or Bleed when the attack hits, deals an additional 450% ATK damage and inflicts &#91;Top&#93; Critical Defense Decrease and &#91;Top&#93; Evasion Decrease, Heal Block for 5 sec.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate – Vanish!
<p>Every 9 sec, deals 1000% ATK damage to all enemies. Increases damage by 50% for each Technique-type ally (up to 350%). After the attack hits, grants all allies &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and Action Impairment Barrier, &#91;High&#93; Shield, &#91;High&#93; Continuous Healing for 5 sec. Extends the duration of these additional effects by 0.5 sec for each Technique-type ally (up to 3 sec).</p>
