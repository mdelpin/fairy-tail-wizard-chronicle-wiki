## Game text

### 🗡️ Normal Skill (Ranged)
<p>Deals 120% ATK damage to the target. After hitting, heals the ally with the lowest HP for 140% of your ATK.</p>

### 🗡️ Special Skill - Command T Expansion
<p>When a criticla hit occurs, for 5 sec, grant all Tech-type allies &#91;High&#93; Critical Rate Increase and &#91;High&#93; Accuracy Increase.</p>

### 💥 Ultimate - Command T Shrink
<p>Every 10 sec, restore HP to the ally with the lowest HP equal to 1250% of your ATK. Then remove all debuffs applied to all allies.</p>
