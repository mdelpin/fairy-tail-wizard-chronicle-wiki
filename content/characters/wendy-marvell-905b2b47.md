## Game text

### Normal Attack (Ranged)
<p>Deals 120% ATK damage to the target.  After hitting, heals the ally with the lowest HP for 140% of your ATK.</p>

### Special Skill – Knights of God
<p>After a normal skill hits, there is a 10% chance to grant allies for 7 sec &#91;Top&#93; ATK Increase and &#91;Top&#93; Critical Rate Increase, and advance the currently accumulated Ultimate SKill Gauge by 0.7 sec.</p>

### Ultimate SKill – Sky Heal
<p>Every 10 sec, restore HP of all allies equal to 800% of own ATK. Then for 7 sec, grant them Blessing of the Sky Dragon</p>
